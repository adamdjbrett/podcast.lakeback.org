Patricia Nolan Vogt (Onondaga descent) is Program Coordinator and Caseworker for the Executive Director of the American Indian Law Alliance.

She has worked for the Onondaga Nation for over 20 years in many capacities, including as a supervisor of several programs, and serves as co-treasurer of the Onondaga Nation Ladies Fire Department Auxiliary. Through her family's teachings she feels a responsibility to uphold and support the Haudenosaunee Confederacy.
