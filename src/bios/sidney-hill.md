Tadodaho Sidney (Sid) Hill is of the Onondaga Nation and holds the title of Tadodaho of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy. Born to Eel Clanmother Phoebe Hill, he was raised in the traditional ways of the longhouse. He was a member of the Iron Workers union, and as a lifelong lacrosse player he was a founding member of the Iroquois National Team and captained it at the 1984 games in Australia.

He was chosen to "warm the seat" of the Tadodaho title in 1996 and was condoled in 2002. It is a lifetime position, and the only one chosen by the other chiefs of the Confederacy. He takes part in discussions at the United Nations on the rights of Indigenous Peoples and has opened the Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues for over ten years.

In Episode 6 he explains his title and the Peacemaker's journey to bring the Great Law of Peace, which was finalized at Onondaga Lake.
