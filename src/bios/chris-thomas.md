Chris Thomas (Onondaga Nation, Beaver Clan) is an acclaimed singer, dancer, and cultural presenter known for his high-energy performances of traditional Haudenosaunee music and dance. Specializing in the Smoke Dance—a fast-paced, competitive Native American dance form celebrated for its complex footwork and rhythms—he shares the rich living heritage of his people with audiences worldwide. Through his dynamic presentations and community leadership, he preserves Indigenous performance traditions while teaching the history, spirit, and athleticism behind Haudenosaunee social dances.
