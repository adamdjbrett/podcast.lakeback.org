Ciarrai Eaton is the Executive Director of the Matilda Joslyn Gage Foundation. She has a background in the arts, with a bachelor's degree in Music and Performance from SUNY Empire, and she worked at the Syracuse City Ballet.

She holds two master's degrees through SUNY Empire: one in Women's History, for which she curated a lecture and exhibition on immigrant women working in Syracuse, and one in Public History, focused on the care of an historic building and the history it embodies. She describes her work at the Gage Foundation as the culmination of her studies and passions.
