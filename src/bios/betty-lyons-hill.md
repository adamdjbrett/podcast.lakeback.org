Gaeñ hia uh, Betty Lyons Hill (Onondaga Nation, Snipe Clan), is President and Executive Director of the American Indian Law Alliance (AILA) and an Indigenous and environmental activist. She has worked for the Onondaga Nation for over 20 years, serves on the Haudenosaunee External Relations Committee, and has taken part in the UN Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues since its first session in 2001 as a delegate of the Onondaga Nation.

She worked closely with AILA from 1997 and joined the organization in 2014, advocating for the Rights of Mother Earth, treaty support, education on the Doctrine of Discovery, and the self-determination of Indigenous nations and peoples at the United Nations. In 2023 she received an honorary degree from Onondaga Community College, and in 2024 a lifetime achievement award from the NYC Bar Association.
