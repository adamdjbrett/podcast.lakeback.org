Sarah Shute is a graduate of SUNY ESF with a background in environmental science. She grew up in the Tully and Lafayette area near Onondaga Lake, and her work with an environmental consulting firm on ecological studies around the lake made her aware of its mercury contamination and unjust history.

She spent several years at the Skä•noñh Great Law of Peace Center, working alongside Tina Thomas on tours and education programs and helping protect the eagles' nest on the lake shore. She serves as Treasurer of the Indigenous Values Initiative.
