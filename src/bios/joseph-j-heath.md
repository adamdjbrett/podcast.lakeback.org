Joseph J. Heath has served as full-time General Counsel for the Onondaga Nation since 1983. His work for the Nation centers on environmental protection, particularly Onondaga Lake and Onondaga Creek under the Clean Water Act, along with treaty rights, hunting and fishing rights, burial site protection, NAGPRA repatriation, and Indian Child Welfare Act cases.

In more than 45 years of practice he has also worked in civil rights litigation, including as one of four lawyers in the class action over the 1971 Attica prison assault, which settled for $12 million in 2000.
