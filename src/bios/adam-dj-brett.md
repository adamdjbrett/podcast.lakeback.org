Adam DJ Brett has been involved with the American Indian Law Alliance since 2016. He earned his Ph.D. in religion at Syracuse University, where he studied religion, media, and culture in the United States.

His interests include the impact of the Doctrine of Discovery, religious freedom, and how religion is understood through law. He facilitates conference discussions and panels and is dedicated to advancing the sovereignty and self-determination of Indigenous nations and peoples.
