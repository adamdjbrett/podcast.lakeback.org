Gwahuntiyosta ("She Has Beautiful Fields"), Tina Hill Thomas, is a member of the Onondaga Nation Eel Clan and lives on the Onondaga Nation. Her professional background is in hotel and restaurant management, and she has catered independently for over ten years, specializing in traditional Haudenosaunee foods.

She worked as an Education Associate at the Skä•noñh Great Law of Peace Center, where she and Sarah Shute led tours and watched bald eagles build the first recorded nest on the shore of Onondaga Lake. She continues her cultural education and outreach work through the American Indian Law Alliance, with a passion for cultural revitalization through food, language, and ceremony.
