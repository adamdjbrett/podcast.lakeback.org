Jake Haiwhagai'i Edwards is a member of the Onondaga Eel Clan who lives on Onondaga Nation Territory and helps carry forward the Longhouse oral teachings. Raised as the eleventh of twelve children among Onondaga elders, he learned their knowledge of history and the natural world, and he served on the Onondaga Council of Chiefs for more than 25 years.

The Grand Council of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy appointed him to the Haudenosaunee External Relations Committee, where he takes part in diplomacy, including work at the United Nations. He sits on the boards of the Seventh Generation Fund for Indigenous Peoples and the Indigenous Values Initiative. He was a leading voice in the 400th anniversary of the Two Row Wampum Campaign, when canoes traveled from Onondaga to the UN in New York City, and he speaks on treaties, the seventh generation, and wampum's influence on the U.S. Constitution.
