## The lake

Onondaga Lake lies in Central New York State. It is the sacred site where Gayanashagowa, the Great Law of Peace, was established. The lake is also known for severe pollution, the result of industrial use that desecrated it.

The Onondaga Nation is one of the six nations of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy. As Keepers of the Central Fire, the Onondaga Nation carries the responsibility of maintaining unity and peace within the Confederacy. The Onondaga Nation has brought a petition to the Organization of American States seeking land rights to the lake.

## The podcast

Healing the Sacred works through story, ceremony, and public scholarship. It addresses the history of Onondaga Lake, its significance to the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, and the damage that industrial pollution has done to it.

The podcast takes its name from an article published in the International Journal on Responsibility, [Healing the Sacred: The Fight to Restore Onondaga Lake and Honor Indigenous Land](https://commons.lib.jmu.edu/ijr/vol9/iss1/2/). The article sets Haudenosaunee teachings beside the European Christian Doctrine of Discovery and shows how that clash of worldviews led to violence, land theft, and genocide against the Haudenosaunee and other Indigenous Nations. Like the article, it makes the case that returning Onondaga Lake to the Onondaga Nation is a vital step toward restoring this sacred place. That return would bring long-overdue healing to its people, the surrounding communities, and the natural world. Protecting and restoring the lake serves the well-being of all.
