*Healing the Sacred* centers Onondaga Lake, a sacred place in the ancestral homelands of the [Onondaga Nation](https://www.onondaganation.org/aboutus/), one of the six nations of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy. We acknowledge the Onondaga Nation as a living, sovereign Nation whose relationship with these lands and waters continues today.

We honor the Onondaga people's enduring care for the land and water, and their continuing responsibilities to future generations. The [Onondaga Nation's account of the lake](https://www.onondaganation.org/land-rights/onondaga-lake/) places its sacred significance and the work of caring for it in the Nation's own words.

We hold that returning Onondaga Lake to the Onondaga Nation is a vital step toward restoring this sacred place and toward healing for its people, the surrounding communities, and the natural world. We support the Nation's sovereignty and self-determination.

We invite listeners to learn from the Onondaga Nation's own accounts and to support the work of returning land and restoring relationships. [LakeBack](https://lakeback.org) shares ways to learn about and support the return of Maple Bay on Onondaga Lake.
