# Source ledger: aila.ngo and lakeback.org (plus the eight Feynman lake packets)

Prepared by the researcher for the *Healing the Sacred* curriculum guide. Checked 14 November 2026.

**How to read this ledger.**
- Every entry was opened as a local file. Every URL was also fetched with `curl -L` (GET, redirects followed). A control request to a made-up path on each site returned 404, so a 200 here is a real page and not a catch-all.
- **Permalink rules used** (read from the site config, not guessed):
  - aila.ngo posts: `/{{ title | slugify }}/`, using the site's own `slugify` options. The slug comes from the post title, not the file name.
  - aila.ngo pages: `/issues/<file>/`, `/programs/<file>/`, `/about/our-history/<file>/`.
  - aila.ngo PDFs: `/assets/documents/<file>.pdf`.
  - lakeback.org: `/timeline/<file>/`, `/blog/<file>/`, `/faq/`, `/pdfs/<file>.pdf`.
- **"Page date"** is the date in the page's front matter. For lakeback.org timeline entries these are sequential placeholders (2025-02-01 to 2025-02-16), so each entry also gives the event date from the page's own label. In Chicago citations I use `n.d.` for timeline entries and undated static pages.
- Many aila.ngo and lakeback.org posts are short excerpts that point to an article elsewhere (Central Current, Syracuse.com, the Daily Orange, the AP). The annotation says so. I did not open those external articles, except the journal article in entry 13.
- Where a source is Haudenosaunee-authored or Nation-adapted I say so. Where it is AILA's campaign voice, I say that too.

Episode key: 1-2 Eagles; 3 Jake Edwards; 4-5 Rex Lyons; 6 Tadodaho Hill; 7-8 Heath; 9 Richard Hill; 10-11 Gage.

## Cautions

**From the Feynman evidence packets (A to H), claims flagged as single-source or as needing the Nation's sign-off**

- Packet A, F2/F3: the date of the district court dismissal. The Toensing article (entry 9) gives September 22, 2010 and the judge's name, so the "month/day not confirmed" gap has one source. It is still a single news report. The Second Circuit dates (Oct 12 and Oct 19, 2012) are single-source and I did not check them.
- Packet A, F6: the 2023 IACHR ruling. The packet calls it an admissibility decision (Report No. 51/23). AILA's 2024 event notice (entry 11) says the Nation was "the first Indigenous nation to achieve the merits stage." These are different claims; do not repeat either without the Nation's counsel confirming.
- Packet A, F7/F9 and packet H: the packets say Resolution No. 452-2011 committed the county to return Murphy's Island and quote Resolution 64 of 2016. Entry 16 shows only a committee vote (4 ayes, 1 abstention, contingent on an amendment) and no resolution number. Entry 17 is the advisory-committee resolution that recites the 2016 support resolution; its wording is "County-owned park property" and "memorialized support," which differs from the packet's quoted words. Use the PDF's own wording, and ask the County Clerk for the 2011 and 2016 adopted texts.
- Packet A, F11 (and the excerpt in entry 23): "first foothold in roughly 200 years" is single-source (Central Current via AILA/LakeBack).
- Packet A, F13/F20 and packet H: the position that Nation land inside 1794 Canandaigua territory is not taxable, and that the 1794 treaty places the lake with the Nation, is the campaign's legal position (entry 15), not a court finding; packet A marks it single-source and says the treaty text was not re-checked. The tax and litigation-waiver terms of any Maple Bay deal also need the Nation's sign-off, and the packet asks that AILA's position be kept separate from the Nation's official position.
- Packet A, F14, F15, F16 and packet G, item 7: quotations tied to the sitting County Executive (the March 2025 letter, the "bargaining chip" and Columbus statue story, back-taxes) should stay as attributed quotation.
