## 5. Do not print

- **2023 decision.** Any wording that calls it a merits ruling or a property ruling. Use the Nation's words, attributed.
- **Dates from the Nation's March 11, 2025 release:** March 28, August 23, 2010, and "10/13/2013." They are wrong.
- **Relief.** "Sought no land" or "did not seek the land back."
- **2011 county action.** "Resolved to return" lake land, and the name of the private citizen behind the 2011 resolution.
- **Unverified Heath claims, unless attributed to him:**
  - Mahoney's rescission request.
  - The committee's last meeting in 2022.
  - The denied motion to intervene.
  - Mercury "daily 1946–1970."
- **Contested figures (DECISIONS 12).** The Murphy's Island acreage, the 90–95% figure and any other contamination figure.
- **Nationals.** "Fifth full member nation," the 1984 Australia captaincy date, and the IOC meeting stated as fact.
- **"Resolution 331"** and the 1911 Albany fire; the Everett report's dates.
- **Gage claims from Eaton:** the suffrage "deal," the Council of Mothers, Mott and London, the temperance amendment, and the Anthony and Stanton break, unless attributed to Eaton.
- **Quotation and name forms.** "Never was justice more perfect" presented as Gage's verdict on Haudenosaunee government. "She Who Carries the Sky" as the Foundation's form.
- **Eagles.** "First in recorded history" stated as fact.
- **Front matter.** The funding and co-production credit, and "Onondaga descent," until confirmed.
- **Citation errors.** The 2003 Book Publishing Co. date for Wagner, "11/16/06" for the Hill declaration, and the "Onondaga Nation's Vision…" bracket on entry 18.
- **Petition PDF.** Any contact details from it.

## 6. Method note

I used Crossref for the journal articles and the Williams and Rodriguez books. Books and dissertations came from the hl and dome libraries: their title, copyright and CIP pages.

For the courts, I used the U.S. Reports PDFs at the Library of Congress for *Sherrill* and *Johnson*. For *Onondaga Nation v. New York*, I used the Caselaw Access Project's static record and the order PDF.
