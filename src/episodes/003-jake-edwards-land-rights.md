## Show notes

In this episode, Gaeñ hia uh, Betty Lyons Hill, Executive Director of the American Indian Law Alliance, interviews Jake Edwards of the Onondaga Nation Eel Clan and discusses the cultural, historical, and environmental significance of Onondaga Lake. Edwards reflects on witnessing industrial pollution as a child, the loss of wildlife and traditional access to the lake, and the ongoing consequences of contamination for the surrounding ecosystem and Haudenosaunee communities. The conversation examines the Great Law of Peace, the [Thanksgiving Address](https://americanindian.si.edu/environment/pdf/01_02_Thanksgiving_Address.pdf) (Ganohé:nio), and the responsibility to consider the well-being of all living beings across seven generations. Edwards also discusses the history of disputed land transactions, the Doctrine of Discovery, and the Onondaga Nation’s efforts to reclaim jurisdiction over its ancestral lands. Ultimately, he emphasizes that environmental protection requires recognizing the interconnectedness of all beings, respecting Indigenous sovereignty, practicing gratitude, and taking responsibility for the water and resources we use.

## Discussion questions

1. How did Jake Edwards’ childhood experiences and his uncle’s teachings shape his understanding of Onondaga Lake and the effects of environmental contamination?
2. How does the Great Law of Peace encourage people to consider the consequences of their decisions for future generations and the natural world?
3. Why does Edwards connect the return of Onondaga Lake to the Onondaga Nation with environmental healing? How might recognizing Indigenous relationships with the land influence approaches to environmental protection?

## Further resources

- [Thanksgiving Address: Greetings to the Natural World](https://americanindian.si.edu/environment/pdf/01_02_Thanksgiving_Address.pdf): PDF from the Smithsonian's National Museum of the American Indian with the words of the Thanksgiving Address in English, for reading alongside the episode.
- [Neighbor to Neighbor, Nation to Nation](https://peacecouncil.net/sites/default/files/NOON-booklet-web.pdf): Free educational booklet (PDF) of readings about the relationship between the Onondaga Nation and Central New York.
- [Onondaga Nation Land Rights Complaint](https://doctrineofdiscovery.org/onondaga-nation-land-rights/): The Nation's 2005 complaint asking for healing between the Onondaga and their neighbors, hosted on doctrineofdiscovery.org.
- [Onondaga Nation v. NY](https://doctrineofdiscovery.org/onondaga-nation-v-ny/): Court decision on the land rights action, based on Sherrill, Cayuga and Oneida.
- [What is the Doctrine of Discovery?](https://outcome.doctrineofdiscovery.org/featured/essay1/what-is-the-doctrine-of-discovery/): Essay by Philip P. Arnold and Sandra Bigtree opening with Faithkeeper Oren Lyons.
- [Focus on the Onondaga: A chronology of dispossession](https://arcg.is/HTDry): Map-based chronology of Onondaga land loss, linked from doctrineofdiscovery.org.
- [Saving a Sacred Lake: A Century of Pollution Haunts the Haudenosaunee](https://aila.ngo/saving-a-sacred-lake-a-century-of-pollution-haunts-the-haudenosaunee/): Overview of the pollution that Mr. Edwards remembers from childhood.
- [Historical Ecology of Onondaga Lake: Timeline](https://onondagalakehistoricalecology.weebly.com/timeline.html): Includes the 1795 New York acquisition of the lake and surrounding land.
- Perreault, Tom, Sarah Wraight, and Meredith Perreault. "Environmental Injustice in the Onondaga Lake Waterscape, New York State, USA." *Water Alternatives* 5(2), 2012, 485-506. Examines how contamination and Onondaga claims to the lake intersect.
- Speak, Larissa. ["The Onondaga Nation's Land Claim: Rights Without a Remedy?"](https://digitalcommons.law.seattleu.edu/ailj/vol13/iss2/2) *American Indian Law Journal* 13, no. 2 (2025). Covers the Marshall cases, Sherrill and the [land rights action](https://www.onondaganation.org/land-rights/).
- Miller, Robert J., Jacinta Ruru, Larissa Behrendt, and Tracey Lindberg. *Discovering Indigenous Lands: The Doctrine of Discovery in the English Colonies*. Oxford University Press, 2010. Legal history of the doctrine.
